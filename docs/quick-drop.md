# Quick drop

Shift + right-click on an item in the inventory (including the extended inventory) drops it on the ground at your feet at once,
without picking it up first. It helps to clear unneeded items quickly.

- Works for items in the bag only, never for equipped gear.
- Uses the same rules as dragging an item to the ground: dead players, drop-banned items, high-value items (jewels, excellent,
  ancient, +7 or higher) and Jewel of Harmony reinforced items are refused with the usual message.
- Does nothing special while a shop, trade, vault, crafting, Auction House, Jewel Bank or similar window is open; right-click keeps its
  usual meaning there.
- One drop is sent at a time: the next click is ignored until the server has answered the previous drop.
